Everything in one place

Built for traders who want the receipts

A complete picture of your trading, derived from an immutable event log you fully control.

Immutable ledger

Every fill is an event. Positions, realized closes, and daily aggregates are all derived, so your history is auditable and reproducible.

FIFO-derived P&L

Realized profit and loss computed with first-in-first-out matching across equities and options, consistent and explainable.

Import & broker sync

Bring trades in via CSV/JSON, or connect a brokerage with optional SnapTrade sync to keep your journal current.

Options & wheel

Dashboards for options analytics and the wheel strategy that track premium, assignments, and outcomes over time.

Performance & charts

Performance dashboards, position views, a trade journal, and charts powered by Chart.js and Lightweight Charts.

Private by design

Self-host on your own database and domain. Your trades, accounts, and keys stay on infrastructure you operate.

The intelligence layer

AI that actually reads your trades

Not a chatbot bolted on top. Three layers of intelligence: a tool-calling agent, on-device machine learning, and an agent-ready API, all working from your real, FIFO-derived ledger.

Ask Joey

A conversational agent that calls real tools, running scoped queries against your ledger to answer questions with actual numbers, not guesses.

Vercel AI SDK · Anthropic / OpenAI

On-device AI Insights

TensorFlow.js models trained on your own history: entry timing, exit strategy, risk, position health and more. Models run on your infra, so your data never leaves.

TensorFlow.js · runs locally

Agent-ready MCP

A read-only MCP endpoint exposes 15+ scoped tools to your personal agents, protected by per-user API keys, never mutating your ledger.

  -H "Authorization: Bearer ajt_••••••••" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "method": "tools/call",
    "params": {
      "name": "get_ticker_recommendation",
      "arguments": { "symbol": "AAPL" }
    }
  }'

A real tool surface

The same scoped queries power Ask Joey and the MCP endpoint.

get_portfolio_summary get_performance get_trade_metrics get_positions get_ai_actions get_ticker_recommendation get_market_regimes get_journal_entries + more

  • Read-only & user-scoped: agents can read, never write.

  • Keys you control: ajt_ keys are shown once, stored as HMAC-SHA-256 hashes.

  • Bring your own provider: Anthropic or OpenAI via env vars.
