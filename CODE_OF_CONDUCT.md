# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Average Joe Trades to be a welcoming, useful project for people with different backgrounds, skill levels, trading styles, and perspectives.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent when clarification is reasonable.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect private financial, identity, account, and credential data.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sharing real credentials, account numbers, or private brokerage data.
- Spam, market manipulation, pump-and-dump coordination, or financial advice presented as guaranteed outcomes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable action to protect the project community.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the maintainers' public support channel. Do not include private financial data or secrets in a public report.
